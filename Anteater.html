How Matching Works
  • Email (counts on its own): the client row has an email that's on exactly one Compass contact. Shared inboxes like info@, help@, office@ never count.
  • No email match? If more than one Compass contact has the name (counting nicknames and small typos), the row is skipped - even if a phone or address points to one of them.
  • Name + phone / Name + address: the one Compass contact with that name also has the same phone number or street address.
  • Exact name: first + last name matches exactly one Compass contact.
  • Nickname / spelling: one half of the name is exact and the other is a nickname (Mike / Michael) or small typo - and no other Compass contact is even close.
  • Never matches when there's no last name, when the row is a company/trust or has two people in it, or when two rows in the client file land on the same Compass contact.
